ComplyTech

About:

This website is a student effort to offer help to companies attempting to achieve compliance with the NCA ECC framework.

Features:

  • Audit Management: This website allows you to create NCA ECC audit projects, invite participants to join your projects, manage your project, and oversee progress through a dashboard. Projects Page
  • Documentation: This website allows you to access the entirety of the NCA ECC domains and sub-domains in an easy-to-use tree. Tree Page
